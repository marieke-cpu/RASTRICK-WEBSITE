§ 05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a project take?+
A single-page site typically ships in 1–2 weeks. A multi-page build takes 3–6 weeks from kickoff to launch. Timelines depend on how quickly content is supplied and the number of revision rounds we go through.
Do you only build custom-coded websites?+
Yes. Every website built by RASTRICK. MADE is custom-coded from scratch using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We don’t build on Wix, Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace, or any page builder platform. This means faster performance, cleaner code, and a website built specifically for your organisation.
Do you redesign Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, or WordPress websites?+
We don’t work inside those platforms — not for edits, redesigns, or maintenance. If your current website is on a platform like Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, or Shopify and you want to work with RASTRICK. MADE, the project becomes a complete custom-coded rebuild. That’s a good thing — it’s faster, more flexible, and built to last.
Can you build for paid ads and campaign traffic?+
Yes. Landing pages and conversion flow are built specifically for ad traffic quality. Message match, CTA hierarchy, mobile speed, and enquiry flow are all structured to lift lead and sales outcomes.
Will my website be mobile-friendly?+
Absolutely. Every website I build is mobile-first and tested across devices and screen sizes. A site that doesn't work perfectly on a phone isn't finished — that's not how I ship.
What do you need from me to start?+
A deposit, any existing brand assets (logo, colours, fonts if you have them), content or copy you want on the site, and a clear idea of what you want it to achieve. No brief is too rough — we'll work through it together on a discovery call.

Ready to build something that actually works?

Tell me about your organisation, your goals, and what you need your website to do. You’ll hear back with a clear recommendation within one business day.
