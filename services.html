§ 03 / Why custom-coded

Why not just
use a website
builder?

Page builders like Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, and Shopify are convenient — but they come with real trade-offs: bloated code, shared infrastructure, limited design control, and ongoing platform fees.

RASTRICK. MADE builds every website from scratch — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No templates. No platform lock-in. No hidden limitations. The result is a faster, more flexible, and more cost-effective website in the long run.

You own the code outright. No monthly platform subscription required to keep your website online. No permission needed to make it yours.

Custom vs platforms
Here's what changes when you go custom-coded instead of building inside a platform.
  • Faster load times — no platform bloat or third-party scripts
  • No monthly CMS or platform subscription fees
  • Complete design control — no template constraints
  • You own the code outright — no platform lock-in
  • Better Core Web Vitals — clean, purpose-built code
  • Easier to maintain and update as your organisation grows
  • No platform rules about what you can and can't build
  • Higher trust perception — especially for professional services
Already on Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, or Shopify? RASTRICK. MADE doesn't work inside those platforms — but if you want to move forward together, the project becomes a complete custom-coded rebuild. That's a fresh start with no compromises.

§ 04 / How it works

The process, clear and focused.

01

Discovery

We define your goals, audience, offer, and what your website needs to do. Scope, structure, and budget are agreed before a pixel is designed.

02

Content planning

Page structure, messaging hierarchy, and content plan are mapped before design begins. What goes on each page and why.

03

Design

A clear visual direction and page-by-page layout is developed — built for trust, clarity, and action aligned to your goals and your audience.

04

Build

Your website is coded from scratch in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — fast, mobile-first, SEO-ready, and built to last without a platform subscription.

05

Review

Two rounds of revisions, cross-device QA, performance testing, and any refinements before we're ready to go live.

06

Launch

Go live with confidence. Domain connection, analytics setup, and post-launch support included so you're not on your own after handover.
